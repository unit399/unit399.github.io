Team Radar

The team picks 5–6 dimensions it cares about — like quality, focus, fun, courage — rates each, and plots a radar. Gaps between ratings spark the honest conversations.

  • 60 min
  • 3–12 people
  • Remote or in person
  • Established team

Steps

  1. 10 min
    Choose dimensions

    Agree 5–6 dimensions the team wants to measure itself on.

  2. 5 min
    Silent rating

    Everyone rates each dimension 1–10, privately.

  3. 10 min
    Plot and observe

    Draw all ratings on the radar; observe averages and spreads.

  4. 25 min
    Discuss the gaps

    Low averages and wide spreads first: why do we see it differently?

  5. 10 min
    Decide actions

    Pick one dimension to move this sprint; agree the action and a target.
