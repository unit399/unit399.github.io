Speed Car

The team is a race car: the engine drives us, the parachute drags us, the abyss ahead is the risk, the bridge is how we cross it.

  • 45 min
  • 3–8 people
  • Remote or in person
  • Any team

Steps

  1. 5 min
    Draw the car

    Sketch car, engine, parachute, abyss, and bridge; explain each.

  2. 12 min
    Silent writing

    Notes for every zone: engines, parachutes, risks, bridges.

  3. 10 min
    Share and cluster

    Place notes on the drawing and cluster similar ones.

  4. 12 min
    Discuss

    Focus on parachutes and the abyss: what slows us, what threatens us?

  5. 6 min
    Build bridges

    Agree 1–2 owned actions that cut a parachute or bridge the abyss.
