Superhero

The team as a superhero: superpowers, kryptonite, arch-nemesis, and trusty sidekick. Reveals identity and dependencies with humor — best with teams that know each other.

  • 60 min
  • 4–10 people
  • In person
  • Established team

Steps

  1. 5 min
    Set the stage

    Draw the hero silhouette with four zones: powers, kryptonite, nemesis, sidekick.

  2. 15 min
    Silent writing

    Everyone fills the four zones with notes about the team.

  3. 15 min
    Share

    Read and cluster; enjoy the character your team becomes.

  4. 15 min
    Discuss

    How do we protect against kryptonite? How do we use our powers more?

  5. 10 min
    Decide actions

    One action to shield kryptonite, one to amplify a power.
