Plus / Delta

The minimal retro: what worked (+) and what we'd change (Δ). Two columns, thirty minutes, zero setup — ideal when time is short.

  • 30 min
  • 2–12 people
  • Remote or in person
  • New team

Steps

  1. 3 min
    Set the stage

    Draw two columns: + and Δ.

  2. 10 min
    Silent writing

    Everyone adds notes to both columns.

  3. 12 min
    Discuss deltas

    Read the pluses quickly; spend the time on the deltas.

  4. 5 min
    Decide actions

    Turn the top delta into one owned action.
